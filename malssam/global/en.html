긁히다 [Geul-ki-da]
Triggered / Salty
When someone gets visibly upset, defensive, or angry because a cold hard truth hit them right in their ego.
A: "Be honest, you're not even 5'8\", right?" B: "Bro why bring up height out of nowhere?! That's so uncalled for!" A: "LMAO look at you, you got totally TRIGGERED! (긁혔네)"
🔥 Context: Gaming banter, K-POP Bubble chats 👍 2,845 Agreed
중꺾마 [Joong-Kkeok-Ma]
Unbreakable Spirit
Abbreviation for "What matters is an unbending heart." An indomitable mentality refusing to surrender against impossible odds. (Originated from Deft at Worlds 2022)
"Grades are down and job interviews are brutal, but we keep pushing with our Joong-Kkeok-Ma spirit!"
👑 Context: Esports, motivational quotes 👍 4,920 Agreed
폼 미쳤다 [Form Mi-chyeot-da]
He is COOKING / Absolute GOAT
Originally from sports "form". Used to praise when an idol's visuals, vocals, skill, or performance are mind-blowingly top tier.
"Did you see our bias in the new comeback MV?! His vocals and visuals are literally COOKING right now!"
🔥 Context: K-POP Comeback, Live streams 👍 4,210 Agreed
갓생 [God-saeng]
Main Character Era / Boss Life
Compound of 'God' + 'Saeng (Life)'. Living an ultra-productive, healthy, and disciplined daily routine (early morning workout, study, reading).
"Woke up at 6 AM, hit the gym, and studied Korean. I'm officially entering my God-saeng era!"
✨ Context: Self-care, Study motivation 👍 4,650 Agreed
