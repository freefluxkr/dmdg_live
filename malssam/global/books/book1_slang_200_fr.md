- **Sens et Origine**: Iconic 1990s PC telecom chat greeting meaning 'Nice to meet you!'

- **Jeu de Rôle K-Culture**: A: Heyo room! B: Hey there!

- **Sens et Origine**: Originally 'Have a pleasant day', turned into cold rejection slang meaning 'get lost'.

### 004. OTL (OTL) — Facepalm / Defeated on all fours
