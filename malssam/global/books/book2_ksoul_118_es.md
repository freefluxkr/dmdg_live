## Parte 1. Texturas de la Mente: Emociones K-Soul (36)

### 001. 서운하다 (seoun-hada) — Seounhada (A gentle emotional disappointment rooted in deep affection)
- **Significado y Origen**: A delicate feeling of feeling let down or neglected by someone close to you. Unlike anger or coldness, it only occurs because you genuinely care about and cherish that person.
- **Dramatización K-Culture**: A: I felt a bit 'seounhada' when my best friend forgot my birthday. / B: It hurts more precisely because you love them so much.


### 002. 답답하다 (dapdaphada) — Dapdaphada (Suffocating mental blockage, chest-tightening frustration)
- **Significado y Origen**: A visceral sensation of feeling mentally choked or having a heavy weight pressing on your chest when progress is stalled, communication fails, or someone is clueless.
- **Dramatización K-Culture**: A: Trying to explain this to someone who refuses to listen is so 'dapdaphada'! / B: Like eating dry sweet potatoes without any water!


### 003. 억울하다 (eogulhada) — Eogulhada (The burning anguish of being falsely accused or treated with injustice)
- **Significado y Origen**: A gut-wrenching ache of acute injustice felt when you are completely blameless yet wrongly blamed, penalized, or misunderstood without a chance to defend yourself.
- **Dramatización K-Culture**: A: I was blamed for a mistake someone else made. It feels so 'eogulhada'! / B: We'll pull the logs and clear your name.


### 004. 여운 (yeoun) — Yeoun (The lingering emotional resonance and poetic aftertaste of an experience)
- **Significado y Origen**: Literally 'lingering sound/rhyme'. The profound emotional reverberation or spiritual afterglow that stays with you long after a book ends, a song stops, or someone leaves.
- **Dramatización K-Culture**: A: I still can't stop thinking about the movie's final scene. / B: It leaves a breathtaking 'yeoun' that stays in your heart for days.


### 005. 심쿵 (simkung) — Simkung (A sudden, breathless heart-flutter triggered by cuteness or charm)

## Parte 2. Lazos de 'Uri': Relaciones y Afecto Coreano (25)

### 001. 정(情) (jeong) — Jeong (Unconditional emotional bond & warm affection built through shared time)
- **Significado y Origen**: Transcendence of mere affection or love. It is an enduring, sticky bond of warm attachment that naturally grows between people, places, or things through shared moments, struggles, and history.
- **Dramatización K-Culture**: A: Even after moving away, I still miss my old neighbors so much. / B: That's 'Jeong'—the deep emotional bond formed through shared life.


### 002. 눈치 (nunchi) — Nunchi (The art of reading the room and sensing unspoken social cues)
- **Significado y Origen**: Much more than EQ or situational awareness. It is the swift, intuitive superpower to gauge others' moods, hidden intentions, and relational atmospheres without a single word being spoken.
- **Dramatización K-Culture**: A: Did you notice the boss was upset? / B: Yes, good thing we have quick 'nunchi'—otherwise we would have asked for a team dinner today!


### 003. 애교 (aegyo) — Aegyo (Delightful cuteness & charming affection to melt hearts)

- **Dramatización K-Culture**: A: He seemed so quiet at first, but now he is so funny! / B: He just has bad 'natgarim' around new faces until he feels safe.
