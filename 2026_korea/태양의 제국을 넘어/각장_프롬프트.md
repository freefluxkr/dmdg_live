### 제1장. 심야의 호출 (Midnight Call)
> 3D CGI animated movie scene, Pixar Disney style. A fierce, righteous prosecutor, Choi Min-hwan, sitting alone in his dimly lit office at midnight. Moody, dramatic lighting, cinematic, 8k.
